# Shorts → subscriber / returning viewer / long-form funnel for a small channel (late 2026)

Channel in scope: "Chô li nói" (Vietnamese JoJo lore/commentary), ~5 weeks old, 86 subs, 22.265 views / 28 days, 89% of views from the Shorts feed, 98,4% new viewers, related-video picker in Studio web returns nothing yet. Research date: 2026-10-31. No paid ads are in scope.

## Q1. What YouTube officially says (2025-2026): related-video link, Shorts→long-form traffic, Shorts subscribers, effect on long-form recommendations, picker eligibility/delay

### Takeaway
YouTube's official line (May 2025 blog, Sept 2026 Creator Insider) is that Shorts and long-form use separate recommendation systems, that Shorts do not hurt long-form, and that the main bridge is (a) the related-video link and (b) a long-standing signal where recommendations consider recent long-form from channels a viewer *watches* in Shorts. YouTube publishes no number for how many Shorts viewers cross to long-form or how Shorts-acquired subscribers behave. No official source documents a delay for the related-video picker after advanced features unlock.

### Cited Findings
- Help Center: a Short can link to one of your own Videos, Shorts or Live uploads. The link shows in the Shorts player as "a clickable link below your channel handle". You set it after upload in Studio → Content → the Short → Related Video → Save. "Adding a related video is only available with advanced feature access." The target must be public or unlisted and follow the Community Guidelines. — [YouTube Help: Add a related video to your Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157?hl=en)
- URLs in Shorts descriptions and comments are non-clickable (anti-spam), so the related-video link is the main built-in way to send someone out of a Short. — [YouTube Help: Sharing links with your audiences](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639?hl=en) (as summarised in search results)
- Official blog, 6 May 2025, "Shorts truths": "we haven't seen evidence of Shorts negatively impacting long-form performance"; YouTube uses separate recommendation systems per format; "shorts can help drive traffic to your long-form content too!"; Shorts "are a powerful tool for reaching new and diverse viewers"; "Shorts can foster a strong sense of community" through replying to comments. The post does not mention subscribers. — [YouTube Blog, 2025-05-06](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/debunking-common-myths-about-youtube-shorts/)
- Todd Beaupré (YouTube discovery lead), Aug 2022: "Recommendations now consider recent long-form videos from channels the viewer watches in Shorts." This followed a VidCon 2022 admission that the system had stopped recommending long-form to Shorts-only watchers, after which Shorts watch history was separated from long-form. **Older than 2024: the bridge is still cited but no newer official update found.** — [Todd Beaupré on X, 2022-08-19](https://x.com/hitsman/status/1560479565197107202?lang=en); [vidIQ coverage](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-recommends-long-form-videos-shorts-viewers/)
- Creator Insider, 1 Sept 2026 ("YouTube Algorithm 2026", Rene Ritchie interviewing Todd Beaupré): recommendations are "pulled for each viewer" at session time; the system learns from subscriber and non-subscriber responses separately; even top videos see subscribers choose to watch only ~10% of the time in the subscriptions row; the top place subscribers watch their channels' videos is the suggested list on the watch page, not the subscriptions feed; viewers on TV want longer videos and series formats; "align channels with audiences" (one channel if the same people would watch both). YouTube stopped push notifications to inactive subscribers in April 2026 (PPC Land timeline). No supporting data was given for these claims. — [PPC Land summary of Creator Insider interview](https://ppc.land/subscribers-skip-90-of-uploads-in-their-feed-youtube-director-says/)
- Rene Ritchie (YouTube Blog, April 2024): at the core both algorithms focus on viewer satisfaction; Shorts recommendations show more variety because a viewer goes through ~two dozen Shorts in the time of two long videos. — [YouTube Blog, Rene's Top Five, 2024-04-11](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/renes-top-five-on-youtube-april-11-2024-edition/)
- Ritchie, quoted by a third party (2025): YouTube looks at "video and topic", not channel, so a weak Short does not hurt the channel; the benefit is that "more people are aware of you" and may recognise you when a long video appears on the home page. — [The Leap](https://www.theleap.co/blog/youtube-shorts-algorithm/) (secondary quote)
- Official viewer segments (introduced 24 July 2025, replacing "returning viewers"): **new** = first-time, or private browser/cleared history/not watched in >1 year; **casual** = watched at least once per month for 1-5 months in the past year; **regular** = watched at least once per month for more than 6 months in the past year. YouTube says regular viewers are often "<1%", common "for newer channels, trending videos, and channels that mostly post Shorts". — [YouTube Help: Understand new, casual & regular viewers](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10246996?hl=en); [PPC Land, July 2025](https://ppc.land/youtube-introduces-refined-viewer-analytics-with-casual-and-regular-metrics/)
- Conflicting third-party claims: some blogs say the Shorts engine was "fully decoupled" from long-form in late 2025 so neither affects the other; another (Dataslayer) says a December 2025 change made Shorts a testing ground for long-form and cut home-feed long-form slots (Mario Joos: 2 long-form recommendations instead of 12). None cite an official YouTube source. — [meikuio.com](https://meikuio.com/youtube-algorithm-2026/); [Dataslayer, Dec 2025](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/youtubes-december-2025-algorithm-update-browse-feed-cut-long-videos-by-80)
- Third-party troubleshooting for an empty/missing related-video field: the mobile Studio app does not expose the field (use studio.youtube.com); signing in as a Brand Account manager who is not the primary owner shows "advanced features aren't available"; Help pages contradict each other on whether the feature needs phone verification or advanced features; region-restricted videos can suppress link surfaces. — [link.boo guide](https://link.boo/guides/youtube-shorts-link-in-description); [wyrld.de guide](https://wyrld.de/en/guides/links-on-youtube)

### Inferences
- With 98,4% new viewers and a channel only ~5 weeks old, the near-zero "regular" share is a definitional artefact: nobody can have watched "at least once per month for more than 6 months" yet. The first meaningful loyalty metric for this channel is the **casual** share (watched in ≥2 separate months), which can only start climbing now that the channel spans two calendar months.
- The Beaupré bridge works on viewers who *watch* (not swipe) the Short, so Shorts on the same JoJo topics as the long videos, with high % viewed, are the ones that feed long-form recommendations. Off-topic viral Shorts will not.
- The empty picker is most likely a search/indexing or account-role issue, not a policy: check on desktop Studio, as primary owner, that target long videos are public/unlisted (not scheduled/private), and that Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility shows advanced features active. No official delay is documented, so if it is still empty after a few days, report through Studio "Send feedback" / Help Community.
- Since subscribers mostly reach a channel's videos via the watch-page suggested list, a long video's best traffic source for subscribers is the end of another of the channel's own videos (series/playlist chaining), not the subscriptions feed.

### Gaps
- No official YouTube figure for the share of Shorts viewers who click the related-video link, or for long-form watch behaviour of subscribers acquired from Shorts. ClipForge claims "YouTube's own data from 2025" shows Shorts-converted subscribers have 4× higher long-form watch time than search-acquired subscribers, with no primary link; treat as unverified. — [ClipForge](https://clip-forge.io/blog/youtube-shorts-distribution-playbook-2026)
- No official statement on any waiting period for the related-video picker after advanced features unlock.
- Quotes attributed to Ritchie (X, early 2026) and Todd Sherman (2025 Creator Summit: "we do not reduce distribution of your long-form content if you post Shorts") come only from an agency blog and could not be checked against originals. — [MarketMaker Mgmt](https://marketmakermgmt.com/blog-list2/do-youtube-shorts-hurt-your-channel)

## Q2. Tactics that measurably raise subscriber conversion and returning viewers for small channels

### Takeaway
The best-supported levers are consistency of topic/format/host, series built on already-proven topics, and Shorts that share topics with the long-form library and link to them. Hard numbers are scarce and mostly from vendors; the official Help Center tips are qualitative. Benchmarks suggest Shorts convert roughly 0,17-0,15% of views to subs, and the click-through on a Short's related-video link averages ~4,5% (vendor figure).

### Cited Findings
- Official YouTube tips for casual/regular viewers: "Try creating content that's more consistent and similar in topic or format, or work with the same host"; "Create a series of videos from topics that have already proven popular with your viewers"; community tools "posts, comments, live premieres/chats, and brand consistency"; respond to comments; "publish content regularly". For new viewers: thumbnails/titles, "keep your channel fresh with uploads and playlists", collaborate with other creators, trends and seasonal events. The page does not mention Shorts or end screens. — [YouTube Help: New, casual & regular viewers tips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13615784?hl=en)
- Beaupré (Sept 2026): viewers with more history on a channel or topic are more likely to be shown its videos, more prominently; TV viewers want longer videos and series. — [PPC Land / Creator Insider](https://ppc.land/subscribers-skip-90-of-uploads-in-their-feed-youtube-director-says/)
- Paddy Galloway study of 3,3 billion views (April 2023, **pre-2024, possibly outdated**): on average long-form converts more subscribers per 10.000 views than Shorts (except channels >1M subs); Shorts favouring 40 s+ that hold viewers; viewed-vs-swiped matters, so the first second must hook. "If you can do them without taking away focus from your long form videos, go for it!" His agency reported 300-500% long-form view jumps in under 6 months on two client channels using Shorts (X, March 2024). — [Paddy Galloway on X, 2023](https://x.com/PaddyG96/status/1646898356419981315); [Paddy Galloway on X, 2024](https://x.com/PaddyG96/status/1767598553294139561)
- vidIQ (2026): Shorts "only convert when they share topics with your long-form library"; recommends a 2:1 or 3:1 Shorts:long ratio, a 70/20/10 mix (teaser / standalone value / personality), and judging Shorts by average % viewed, swipe-away rate and subscribers gained per Short rather than raw views. — [vidIQ: Shorts for brands](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-for-brands/)
- Benchmarks (vendor/secondary, unverified): ~16,9 new subs per 10.000 Shorts views (0,169%) (Voomo 2025); average CTR from Short to linked long video 4,5% (Zebracat); series videos get 67% higher subscriber conversion than standalone (Swydo 2025 agency data); 0,8-1,5 subs per 1.000 Shorts views is "strong" for educational channels (ClipForge). — summarised in search results citing [Youtowire](https://www.youtowire.com/blog/youtube-shorts-vs-long-videos-subscribers), [ClipForge](https://clip-forge.io/blog/youtube-shorts-distribution-playbook-2026), [Virvid](https://virvid.ai/blog/turning-shorts-views-into-subscribers-2026)
- Creator anecdotes: one creator with 30M+ Shorts views and 100.000+ subs in 6 months says ~60%+ of Shorts viewers won't move to long-form; another lost "most of those new subscribers" every time they posted long-form until Shorts and long-form topics were aligned. — [bbautista Substack](https://bbautista.substack.com/p/my-failed-experiment-with-youtube); [youtubehowto Substack](https://youtubehowto.substack.com/p/are-youtube-shorts-worth-it)
- Shorts rarely show prominently in subscription feeds and almost never trigger a bell notification (blog claim). — [Flintzy](https://www.flintzy.com/blog/youtube-shorts-not-getting-views-heres-why-your-subscribers-arent-watching-and-what-to-do-about-it/)

### Inferences (applied to "Chô li nói")
- The channel's own numbers already match the official tip: subscribers watch 67 s/view vs 42 s for non-subscribers, so the recurring host (Jolie/"Chô li") and fixed JoJo format are working; the job is to make more viewers see the channel twice.
- 86 subs from ~22.265 views in 28 days (plus earlier views) is in the 0,2-0,4% band, at or above the vendor 0,17% Shorts benchmark; the bigger gap is long-form crossover (related video 0,1%).
- Practical stack, ordered by evidence strength: (1) every Short gets a related-video link to the long video on the same JoJo topic once the picker works; (2) Shorts carved from or teasing a long video's mystery, with the payoff explicitly "in the long video" (a curiosity gap the Short does not close); (3) named recurring series (e.g. a numbered "hồ sơ"/"nếu…" series) with a playlist per series, since the watch-page suggested list is the top subscriber traffic source and TV (18% of watch time here) favours series; (4) end screens + pinned comment pointing to the long video on long uploads (pinned links in Shorts are not clickable); (5) reply to comments and use community posts/polls to pick the next topic (official "community" tip).
- Long videos that "start slow then surge" (4.572 views in 48 h days after upload) fit Beaupré's description of per-viewer pull: tests continue after the first hour. Linking new Shorts to an already-surging long video is likely the highest-return use of the related-video slot.

### Gaps
- No credible, recent public dataset isolates the effect of pinned comments, community posts or end screens on subscriber conversion for small channels; numbers above are vendor-sourced and could not be traced to primary methodology.
- No Film Booth or vidIQ primary study on Shorts→subscriber conversion from 2025-2026 was found in this pass.
- No Vietnam-specific conversion data found.

## Q3. One channel mixing Shorts + long-form, or split?

### Takeaway
Current evidence favours keeping both on one channel when the Shorts and the long videos serve the same audience and topic (YouTube's own "align channels with audiences"), with Shorts kept to roughly a quarter to a third of uploads in topic-led niches. Splitting only makes sense if the Shorts audience is genuinely different.

### Cited Findings
- Beaupré (Creator Insider, Sept 2026): "align channels with audiences" — one channel if the same people would watch both; separate brands when audiences differ. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/subscribers-skip-90-of-uploads-in-their-feed-youtube-director-says/); also cited by [YTGrowth](https://ytgrowth.io/blog/shorts-vs-long-form)
- AIR Media-Tech, 18.000 English-language channels, March 2025-March 2026 (≥40 videos, ≥10 long), median subscribers, correlational: mixing correlates with more subs in fitness (+41%), business (+39%), education (+37%), food (+31% at smaller sizes); in gaming long-only channels have 85% more subs; best Shorts share 25-40% of uploads, long-form performance drops above ~55%; 1.247 channels that shifted >30 points toward Shorts in late 2025 did poorly. In entertainment the 100K-1M tier was roughly equal (long-only 73.600 vs mix 72.400 median subs). Its advice: small channels can test Shorts freely; larger channels treat Shorts as secondary discovery. Anime not covered. — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/do-youtube-shorts-help-your-long-form-videos-grow-data-from-18000-channels)
- Metricool 2026 (799.718 videos, 71.177 accounts): channels posting no Shorts averaged 27,08% below the study average on long-form views; ~4,91 Shorts/month → 46,86% above; ~35 Shorts/month → 25,90% below. Correlational. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- YouTube: no evidence Shorts hurt long-form (May 2025). — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/debunking-common-myths-about-youtube-shorts/)
- Shorts RPM is 3-14% of long-form RPM in almost every niche (AIR, 274 channels). — [AIR Media-Tech (via search summary)](https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/should-you-chase-shorts-views-or-double-down-on-long-form-for-channel-growth)

### Inferences
- "Chô li nói" Shorts and long videos are the same topic (JoJo lore), the same host and the same language, so the Beaupré test says one channel. Splitting would also reset the related-video bridge (it only links to your own channel) and the "watches in Shorts → recent long-form" signal.
- Currently the channel is Shorts-dominant in views; both large datasets suggest that very high Shorts volume relative to long-form correlates with weaker long-form, so the mix should not tilt further toward Shorts-only output.

### Gaps
- No dataset for anime/commentary niches, for non-English or Vietnamese channels, or for channels under ~1.000 subs (AIR's smallest tier is 100K+ monthly-views-based; Metricool doesn't segment by size in what was retrieved).

## Q4. Cadence: Shorts vs long per week for comparable small channels

### Takeaway
Data points to a moderate cadence: about 2-4 Shorts per week maximises per-Short results, ~1-1,5 Shorts per day (≈5 per month in Metricool's split, 25-40% of uploads in AIR's) is where long-form lift appears; more volume raises total Shorts views but correlates with weaker long-form. YouTube says posting frequency itself is not a ranking factor.

### Cited Findings
- Metricool 2026: per-Short views/interactions peak at 2-4 Shorts/week (5.872 views, 130 interactions per Short) with 0,5-1/week close behind (5.812 views); ≥7/week gives 5.078 views per Short; total monthly views keep rising with frequency; average posting rose from 2,26 to 2,72 per week 2025→2026 (spans the March 2025 view-count change). — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- AIR: Shorts 25-40% of uploads optimal in topic niches; >55% hurts long-form. — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/do-youtube-shorts-help-your-long-form-videos-grow-data-from-18000-channels)
- vidIQ: 2:1 to 3:1 Shorts:long ratio. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-for-brands/)
- Todd Sherman (2023, **pre-2024**): posting frequency and timing don't necessarily boost performance. — [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/news/23/08/34020532/youtube-shorts-chief-debunks-popular-myths-no-posting-10-videos-a-day-doesnt-affect-your-performance)
- Official tip: "publish content regularly". — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13615784?hl=en)
- Vendor claim (no source): 15+ Shorts/month grow 32% faster; hybrid channels 41% faster. — [LongStories.ai](https://longstories.ai/blog/shorts-boost-long-form-video-performance) (unverified)

### Inferences
- For a one-person, quality-gated Vietnamese channel (each Short needs storyboard approval), a sustainable target consistent with the data is ~3-4 Shorts + 1 long video per week (≈3:1, Shorts ~75% of uploads by count but long-form dominating effort/watch time); if output is tight, cut Shorts count before long videos, and keep a fixed weekday slot for the long video so the series feels regular.
- The channel's own analytics (1-min Shorts at 56-63% avg viewed beat 2-3 min ones) should outweigh generic cadence data when choosing format; cadence data only says "don't flood".

### Gaps
- No cadence data for channels under 1.000 subscribers, for Vietnam, or for anime/commentary specifically. Metricool's cadence table is per-Short performance, not growth of subscribers.
- Vietnam context only: Google/Kantar "Future of Video 2026" says YouTube reaches 93% of Vietnamese internet users daily and over 50% of watch time on Vietnamese content comes from international audiences (YouTube Festival Vietnam, 16 Sept 2026); no Shorts-specific Vietnam numbers found. — [vietnam.vn](https://www.vietnam.vn/en/nen-tang-youtube-tiep-can-toi-93-nguoi-dung-internet-viet-nam-moi-ngay)

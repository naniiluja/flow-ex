# JoJo topics hotly debated by Chinese-speaking fans (Oct 2025 – Oct 30, 2026)

Research note, 2026-10-30. Sources are Chinese-language web pages (Bilibili, Zhihu, Sohu, SMZDM, Bahamut, Douban mirrors, 233乐园). **Caveat on SMZDM:** both SMZDM pages (post.smzdm.com/p/a956v6v0, /p/al37k2l8) are labelled "内容由AI生成" aggregates of 40+ sources. Their Bilibili like/comment numbers are reported here as "per SMZDM aggregate" and were NOT re-verified on Bilibili, except where noted. Only one Bilibili page was fetched directly (BV1igaz6DETi).

## Q1. What JoJo content is popular / debated on Bilibili, Zhihu, Tieba, Weibo, Baha?

### Takeaway
In the last 12 months, nearly all Chinese JoJo debate centres on the **Steel Ball Run (飙马野郎) anime** (Netflix-exclusive, ep 1 on 2026-03-19, weekly from 2026-09-25). The biggest recurring Bilibili format is a **per-episode "删改盘点" (cuts audit) series** comparing anime and manga. Other threads are the "corny" ED, the half-year gap after ep 1, and an old "SBR is overrated" (德不配位) argument. Tieba and Weibo turned up no citable hot posts.

**Candidate topic E: "Araki ate his own setting" (荒木吃设定/吃书), a Chinese fan term**
- "吃设定/吃书" means silently dropping or contradicting established canon. It appears directly in the ep4 audit title (above).
- An ifeng article argues that forgetting a setting ≠ eating it: "设定前后矛盾，导致无法自圆其说才是吃设定" (only a contradiction the story can't explain counts as "eating" a setting). Classic examples it cites:
  - Star Platinum's range exceeding 2 m in the prison (Part 6).
  - Gold Experience's damage reflection disappearing.
  - DIO using Hermit Purple early in Part 3.
  - DIO's bone relic changing from skull to finger bone in Part 6, and its origin changing from Pucci bringing it back from Egypt to DIO giving it.
  - The article is older and undated in the results; treat it as background — [ifeng](https://feng.ifeng.com/c/7vkBmIHa0hI)

## Q2. SBR anime status and availability in China; JoJoLands

### Takeaway
SBR is **Netflix-global-exclusive**: ep 1 (47 min) aired 2026-03-19, and 11 weekly episodes started 2026-09-25, so roughly ep 6–7 are out by Oct 30. No legal mainland-China stream was found; Bilibili has only PVs, fan edits and apparently unauthorised full uploads. That contrasts with Stone Ocean (2021–22), which Bilibili licensed but censored.

### Cited Findings
- Netflix exclusive; new voice cast: Sugita Tomokazu as Funny Valentine, Maeno Tomoaki as Mountain Tim, Hikasa Yoko as Hot Pants — [SMZDM](https://post.smzdm.com/p/al37k2l8/); [Baha forum](https://forum.gamer.com.tw/C.php?bsn=107&snA=6215)
- Bilibili has unauthorised-looking uploads labelled "4K高码中字 持续更新" ("4K high-bitrate, Chinese subs, ongoing"), plus pirate sites aimed at overseas Chinese viewers — [search summary](https://www.jianshu.com/p/617ecc5612db); [olevod](https://olevod.com/index.php/vod/detail/id/80594.html)
- Stone Ocean precedent:
  - Bilibili bought the mainland exclusive. Ep1 lost about 3 minutes (24:30 runtime), with scenes cut or redrawn — [Bilibili article cv14270072](https://www.bilibili.com/read/cv14270072/); [Wanuxi](https://www.wanuxi.com/%E3%80%8Ajojo%E7%9A%84%E5%A5%87%E5%A6%99%E5%86%92%E9%99%A9-%E7%9F%B3%E4%B9%8B%E6%B5%B7%E3%80%8B%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BDbilibili%E7%89%88%E5%92%8C%E5%85%A8%E7%90%83netflix%E7%89%88%E5%B7%AE%E5%BE%88%E5%A4%A7/)

- Older AI controversy (**June 2025, before the window**): the Kishibe Rohan film's AI-generated score drew criticism, and on June 14 the music team released a 16,000-character statement — [Zhihu](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/1921929127432271380)

## Q3. Chinese fan nicknames and memes

### Takeaway
Core Chinese memes are 空耳 (mishearing) chants: 砸瓦鲁多 (the "Za Warudo" time stop), 欧拉欧拉 (Ora ora), 木大木大 (Muda muda) and 呀嘞呀嘞daze (Yare yare daze). There are also name puns, such as 空条蕉太狼 (Jotaro as a Pleasant Goat wolf), and the fandom label JO厨 (JoJo fan).

### Cited Findings
- 砸瓦鲁多 is the Chinese mishearing of DIO's "The World". It is used jokingly for any pause, such as pausing a video or an esports pause — [Zhihu/163 summary](https://m.163.com/dy/article/ESC05QU00535AG5Z.html); [BUFF163](https://buff.163.com/news/49120)
- 欧拉欧拉 means "beating someone up" and has become a byword for Jotaro and for "simple, brutal, super-strong attacks". 木大木大 is DIO's line, roughly "useless" — [163](https://m.163.com/dy/article/ESC05QU00535AG5Z.html)
- 空条蕉太狼: Jotaro's nickname, from the wolf 蕉太狼 in the cartoon 喜羊羊 (Pleasant Goat). Other memes in the same answers: 呀嘞呀嘞daze and 白金之星 — [Zhihu](https://www.zhihu.com/question/318607620)
- JO厨 means JoJo fan, as in the headline "JO厨狂喜" ("JoJo fans go wild") — [163](https://m.163.com/dy/article/ESC05QU00535AG5Z.html)
- Moegirl: Jotaro's design was modelled on Clint Eastwood (from Araki's 25th-anniversary book) — [Moegirl TW](https://zh.moegirl.tw/%E7%A9%BA%E6%9D%A1%E6%89%BF%E5%A4%AA%E9%83%8E)
- Stand-name translation: Chinese wikis keep the Japanese originals, e.g. 黄金体验 for Gold Experience, while the Western official release renamed it Golden Wind. Old mainland translations differ from current ones: 红色魔术师 became 魔术师之红 (Magician's Red), and 乔罗·乔班纳 became 乔鲁诺·乔巴纳 (Giorno Giovanna) — [Huiji wiki](https://jojo.huijiwiki.com/wiki/Project:%E8%AF%91%E5%90%8D%E8%A1%A8); [Baidu Baike](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E7%99%BD%E9%87%91%E4%B9%8B%E6%98%9F/10120480)
- 233乐园 slang: "送人头" means feeding kills (used of Lucy), and "德不配位" means its fame outstrips its merit — [233乐园](https://www.233leyuan.com/post-detail/2057876748015288320)

### Inferences
- 砸瓦鲁多 used as a "pause" joke maps neatly onto topic C: "Netflix used Za Warudo on SBR for six months."

### Gaps
- Moegirl's own 砸瓦鲁多 and 欧拉欧拉 entries were not fetched; the definitions come from 163, Zhihu and BUFF.
- No 2025–26 Chinese meme specific to SBR was found (e.g. a nickname for Johnny or Valentine).
